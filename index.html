The Formal Perplexity Equation
PPL(W) = P(w₁, w₂, ..., w_N)-1/N = exp( - 1/N · Σ ln P(wᵢ) )
  1. Tokenization: The sentence is split into \(N\) individual words.
  2. Negative Log-Likelihood (Surprisal): For each word \(w_i\), we compute \(-\ln P(w_i)\). Rare words yield high surprisal!
  3. Average Cross-Entropy: Average the log surprisal over all \(N\) tokens: \(H = -\frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^N \ln P(w_i)\).
  4. Exponentiation: Final Perplexity is \(\text{PPL} = e^H\). It represents the effective branching factor—how many words the model is equally uncertain between.
